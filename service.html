06 / Questions

A little clarity
before we start.

Have a question about your specific project? Send us a message and tell us what you have in mind.

Ask a Question ↗
What do you need from me to get started?
A short description of your business, your goals, the pages or features you need and any existing brand assets. Reference websites can also help explain your preferred direction.
How long does a website take?
The timeline depends on the number of pages, functionality, content readiness and review rounds. We agree on a schedule after discussing your scope.
Can you redesign an existing website?
Yes. We can review your current website, identify what needs improving and plan the design and development work around your priorities.
Will the website work on mobile?
Responsive layouts are included in the example packages. We check the agreed pages at different screen sizes before launch.
Can you add animations?
Yes. We can plan scroll reveals, hover interactions and other motion effects as part of the project, with support for reduced motion preferences.
Can you help after the website launches?
Ongoing maintenance can be arranged separately. We agree on the updates, support coverage and responsibilities before the service begins.
